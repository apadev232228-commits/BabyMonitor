Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 22, 2026 • Effective Date: March 22, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Baby Monitor for Parents (the "Application", package: apadev232228.babymonitor) for Android mobile devices, created by apadev232228. This service is designed for monitoring infants and toddlers securely within a local home network environment.

1. Information We Collect (Local Only)

The Application requires access to your device's Camera and Microphone to function. This access is used solely to stream live audio and video to other devices connected to your own local Wi-Fi network.

  • No Cloud Storage: Audio and video data stream strictly peer-to-peer over your local Wi-Fi router or mobile hotspot.
  • No Server Uploads: We do not upload, record, or store any of your private baby audio or video feeds on external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

The Application uses standard third-party services for app distribution and core support:

  • Google Play Services: Required for application distribution, security, and integrity verification.
  • AdMob: Used to display advertisements in free mode. AdMob may collect and use data including your device's advertising ID and limited diagnostics to provide relevant ads.
  • Google Play Billing: Used for processing Pro version upgrades. Payment information is handled exclusively by Google Play; we do not collect or store your payment details.

3. Local Network Usage

The Application acts as a local MJPEG video and PCM audio server directly on your device. Data transmitted over your local Wi-Fi network is unencrypted by default (HTTP). We recommend using a password-protected, private home Wi-Fi network to ensure your local stream is protected from unauthorized local access.

4. Data Deletion & Retention

We do not collect or retain personal user accounts or video logs on external servers. Any preferences or settings are stored strictly locally on your device and can be erased immediately by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android Settings.

If you have any questions about data or wish to request deletion of any diagnostics associated with your advertising ID (handled by AdMob), contact us at: apadev232228@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

5. Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to our processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by us.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, please contact us via email at: apadev232228@gmail.com
